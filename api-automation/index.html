Frequently asked questions

Can you integrate two systems that do not have a native connector?

Yes, when both systems expose suitable APIs, webhooks, exports, or another reliable interface.

How are duplicate records prevented?

Stable source and destination identifiers, idempotency rules, lookup checks, timestamps, and reconciliation logs are used where supported.

Can the integration run automatically?

Yes. It can be event-driven through webhooks or scheduled at a suitable interval, depending on the systems involved.

Will errors be visible?

Yes. Production-ready workflows should include structured logs, retry behavior, failed-item visibility, and clear recovery steps.
