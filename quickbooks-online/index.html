Frequently asked questions

Do you need access to the QuickBooks file?

For reports pulled directly from QBO, appropriate accountant or invited-user access is normally required. For analysis, an exported report may be sufficient.

Can the report show individual invoice numbers?

Yes. Detailed AR aging can include customer, invoice number, due date, open balance, and aging bucket.

Can you provide both PDF and Excel?

Yes. A polished PDF can be delivered for reading, with an Excel file containing the detailed data and calculations.

Can this become a repeatable monthly process?

Yes. The report setup, export method, workbook refresh steps, and management outputs can be documented and automated where possible.
