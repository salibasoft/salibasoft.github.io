What this service covers

Every model should explain how the result was produced. Assumptions, calculations, scenarios, and outputs are separated clearly so decision-makers can understand what changes the answer.

  • DCF valuation, WACC, terminal value, and sensitivity tables
  • Revenue, cost, cash flow, balance sheet, and funding forecasts
  • Budget versus actual and management KPI dashboards
  • Market sizing, company benchmarking, and scenario models
  • Pricing, break-even, profitability, and investment analysis
  • Excel models, Power BI dashboards, PDF reports, and executive summaries

Frequently asked questions

Will the formulas and assumptions be visible?

Yes. Transparency is a core requirement. Inputs, assumptions, calculations, and outputs are separated and documented.

Can you work from incomplete historical data?

Yes, provided the gaps are identified. Assumptions and estimation methods will be stated clearly rather than hidden.

Can the model include sensitivity analysis?

Yes. Key variables can be tested individually or in two-dimensional tables so users can see what drives the result.

Can you deliver an executive report with the model?

Yes. The detailed workbook can be accompanied by a concise Word or PDF briefing with charts, conclusions, and methodology.
