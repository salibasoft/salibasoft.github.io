Frequently asked questions

Will the workbook contain hidden or locked formulas?

Only when specifically requested. The default approach is transparent and editable, with formulas, assumptions, and tables available for future changes.

Can an existing workbook be improved?

Yes. Existing formulas, VBA, layout, data structure, and performance can be inspected before targeted improvements are made.

Can Excel connect to QuickBooks or an API?

Yes. Depending on access and the source, data can be imported through supported exports, Power Query, REST APIs, or an intermediate structured file.

Can the workbook support new vehicles, customers, or categories?

Yes. Tables, formulas, validation, and dashboard ranges can be designed to expand when new records are added.
